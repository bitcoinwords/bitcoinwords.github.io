# [The economics of sinking 20 MB Gavincoin blocks.](http://www.contravex.com/2015/02/11/the-economics-of-sinking-20-mb-gavincoin-blocks)
### By [Pete Dushenski](http://www.contravex.com/)
### Posted February 11, 2015

Since there still seems to be a handful of holdouts who disagree that [a bigger blockchain is less secure and that the block size ain’t gonna increase any time soon](https://contravex.com/2014/10/07/how-a-bigger-blockchain-is-less-secure-and-why-block-size-aint-gonna-increase-any-time-soon/), let’s do some math to further the point, shall we ?

Of the last six blocks mined in the last 41 minutes,<sup>[i](http://www.contravex.com/2015/02/11/the-economics-of-sinking-20-mb-gavincoin-blocks#footnote_0_2778)</sup> the average block size was 161.09 kB and the average number of transactions was 347. This wasn’t what was ideal, what it “should be” in order for Bitcoin to be the African Visa card “[the community](https://contravex.com/2014/11/21/the-community-will-bust-your-balls-if-you-dont-listen-to-them-and-break-your-bank-account-if-you-do/)” dreams of, this is what it was. **This is what the market decided.**

![](/assets/images/2015/m2/the-economics-of-sinking-20-mb-gavincoin-blocks1.png)

 Take a good hard look at what the people with the money to make decisions *actually* did, not what the [people without the money](https://contravex.com/2014/12/03/to-a-redditard/) thought they should do because reasons. In case you haven’t heard, this is how the world works today and going forward into the foreseeable future. Democratic socialism is dead, we’re just making the necessary funeral arrangements.

But back to the math. So you want spam-a-licious 20 MB blocks and some arbitrary number of transactions per second because you think that’s a meaningful measurement of anything. Fine. Let’s run the numbers and see what that’s going to cost you. Because yes, this decision bears costs, no matter what USGavin says and no matter what he or anyone else imagines to be a “[free lunch](https://contravex.com/2015/01/12/bitcoin-doesnt-need-a-hard-fork-it-needs-hard-people/)“, there are expenditures implied by his proposal and consequences you may find undesirable flowing therefrom.

Currently, the Bitcoin blockchain is growing at a rate of ~1.2 GB every single month.<sup>[ii](http://www.contravex.com/2015/02/11/the-economics-of-sinking-20-mb-gavincoin-blocks#footnote_1_2778)</sup> **Gavincoin’s 20 MB blocks** **would grow the size of its blockchain by 2.88 GIGABYTES PER DAY or 1.05 TERABYTES PER YEAR** since they will be filled with essentially zero-fee<sup>[iii](http://www.contravex.com/2015/02/11/the-economics-of-sinking-20-mb-gavincoin-blocks#footnote_2_2778)</sup> spam transactions *by design*.

If you’re hosting your Gavincoin node on a VPS, you’re looking at costs in the order of $1,000 per year, or *[50x what a Bitcoin node costs](https://contravex.com/2014/10/10/guide-to-setting-up-a-remote-bitcoin-node-for-20-per-year/)!* Even if you’re running your Gavincoin node at home, and you want to buy 5 years of storage up-front, you’re looking at $300 for the hard drive and another $250 per year in additional Internet bandwidth capacity, which is again, about 50x more than you’re currently paying.

This little bit of math should make it completely, utterly, painstakingly obvious that imposing such costs on nodes (not miners now) will reduce the overall number of nodes in the network and fragilise the distributed model that makes Bitcoin the world-eater that it is. This weakness in the Gavincoin model can and will be exploited by state-level actors, those of us in Bitcoin<sup>[iv](http://www.contravex.com/2015/02/11/the-economics-of-sinking-20-mb-gavincoin-blocks#footnote_3_2778)</sup> who intend to enforce by any and all means the doctrine of scarcity until our dying breath, and of course those miners who want to protect their transaction fee revenue for down the road when the block reward dries up.

Gavincoin can and will be defeated because it’s meaningfully worse than the alternative, just as USD, “property values,” and whatever [other unit of account](https://contravex.com/2014/02/23/dogederps/) you previously found to be useful can and will be defeated. Lesser options don’t overcome better options just for the saying so of “the people.”
