### By [Pete Dushenski](http://www.contravex.com/)
### Posted October 7, 2014

Just last night, our good friends over at Qntra posted [Gavin Andresen Proposes Scalability Roadmap and Hardfork](http://qntra.net/2014/10/gavin-andressen-proposes-scalability-roadmap-and-hardfork/). I enjoyed the article and took it upon myself to call out Teh Lead Derp of [Teh Bitcoin Fundation](https://contravex.com/2014/04/07/analysis-annual-letter-from-bitcoin-fundation-chairman-vehehes/) scammers on my second favourite<sup>[i](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/07/how-a-bigger-blockchain-is-less-secure-and-why-block-size-aint-gonna-increase-any-time-soon#footnote_0_1489)</sup> channel for doing so, Twitter:

https://twitter.com/pete_d/status/519364709451968512

When Andresen finally rolled out of bed and read his Notifications this morning, he chimed in on the comments section of Qntra’s article, saying:

1. This blog is #1.[↩](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/07/how-a-bigger-blockchain-is-less-secure-and-why-block-size-aint-gonna-increase-any-time-soon#identifier_0_1489)
2. While there are various types of possible attacks, the most significant is the “51% attack,” wherein a pool of mining power gains the ability to double-spend bitcoins.[↩](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/07/how-a-bigger-blockchain-is-less-secure-and-why-block-size-aint-gonna-increase-any-time-soon#identifier_1_1489)
3. Or just look at the size of the base of the Burj Khalifa vs. your average 15-story apartment building.[↩](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/07/how-a-bigger-blockchain-is-less-secure-and-why-block-size-aint-gonna-increase-any-time-soon#identifier_2_1489)
4. And nearly triple what it was a year ago.[↩](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/07/how-a-bigger-blockchain-is-less-secure-and-why-block-size-aint-gonna-increase-any-time-soon#identifier_3_1489)
5. And it’s one that further affirms the need to keep blocks small.[↩](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/07/how-a-bigger-blockchain-is-less-secure-and-why-block-size-aint-gonna-increase-any-time-soon#identifier_4_1489)
