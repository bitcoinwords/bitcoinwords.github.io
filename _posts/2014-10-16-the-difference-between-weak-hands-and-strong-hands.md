 You’re panicking over the bloodbaths.<sup>[i](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_0_1592)</sup>

STRONG HANDS:
 We’re loving every minute of the big bad bear.<sup>[vii](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_6_1592)</sup>
 We’re buying your bitcoins.
 We’re writing 3000 lines a day.<sup>[viii](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_7_1592)</sup>
 We’re airgapping.<sup>[ix](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_8_1592)</sup>
 We’re running old hardware.<sup>[x](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_9_1592)</sup>
 We’re not updating software.<sup>[xi](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_10_1592)</sup>
 We’re using PGP.<sup>[xii](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_11_1592)</sup>
 We’re rolling dice.<sup>[xiii](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_12_1592)</sup>
 We’re fixing the holes in WordPress’ head.<sup>[xiv](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_13_1592)</sup>
 We’re fending off DDoS attacks.<sup>[xv](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_14_1592)</sup>
 We’re laughing off USG attacks.<sup>[xvi](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_15_1592)</sup>

And we’re calling the shots. After all, we’re La Serenissima.<sup>[xvii](http://www.contravex.com/2014/10/16/the-difference-between-weak-hands-and-strong-hands#footnote_16_1592)</sup>

That’s the difference.
