# [Hello. I am a bitcoin.](https://elaineou.com/2015/05/12/hello-i-am-a-bitcoin/)
### By [Elaine Ou](https://twitter.com/elaineou)
### Posted May 12, 2015



Hello. I am a bitcoin. This is the story of my journey.

I was born on September 17, 2013 at 7:53 am. I came to life in a BTC Guild mining pool, a reward for mining block #258798 in the blockchain. Bitcoin was trading at $126 on my birthday, and [25 of us bitcoins were generated that moment](https://blockchain.info/tx/cfc8c93395036cb37bd06a55a7f537112fc8261511f6c84b92c384f155a5d09d).

In a mining pool, multiple clients contribute computing power to generate a block in a group-copulation sort of way. That meant that ten minutes after birth, all of us were [separated and distributed amongst the miners](https://blockchain.info/address/1K2mSC8tAvWn6epjXqfs9jX1rrKfs38mMm).

I was actually just 0.1 bitcoin. Well really, I’m not a bitcoin, because a bitcoin is not a tangible thing like those [Federal Reserve Notes you call money](https://elaineou.com/2015/02/02/what-is-the-intrinsic-value-of-bitcoin/). I’m more like a bank account balance, where each transaction confers the right to transfer that balance to a new receiver. This story is a trace of transactions.

I sat in the miner’s wallet for ten days. On September 29, [I was thrown into a mixer](https://blockchain.info/address/1NfhBgHpPuEh3rvbFQ6mi3KtZPuFxtNvFD) for [6 hours](https://blockchain.info/address/1LvL9cVNGk7ua6zU9k6bdzjtNU6kJiZvnN).

Bitcoin mixing is the process of combining a user’s funds with that of many others so it becomes impossible to tell where a transaction originated. It looks something like this:

![](/assets/images/2015/m5/hello-i-am-a-bitcoin2.png)

*mixing it up*

This is money-laundering, of course. But there are plenty of legitimate reasons for seeking anonymity! Many people simply don’t want their spending history on public display*.

Virgin bitcoins like myself are particularly valuable for mixers – *virgin* meaning fresh from the coinbase, with no transaction history.

After 6 hours of tumbling, it became this:

![](/assets/images/2015/m5/hello-i-am-a-bitcoin4.png)

We rinsed and [repeated](https://blockchain.info/address/1CZSu1qnxzkxoFtr5HBeRSrpp89Wx3mSaL) for the [next](https://blockchain.info/address/13bRdxgQWfN1w29rMjbasyg4n6vAnqgAjw) [two days](https://blockchain.info/address/1CcvBjDZvwwk9DE3HPtZqdWJ25T58izBmd), lending my clean name to what were possibly tainted funds.

Finally, I was put up for [sale on Mt Gox](https://blockchain.info/tx/f4a6cd62c6d7229e28fb1b68e171c0358d35d5af2c795ede86943af71e517fac) on October 2, 04:33 am. I was [purchased 20 minutes later](https://blockchain.info/tx/96645d6bc81467a27133d895882e2743729cc24f2c15ad9dda807941325bd0c1), and [transferred into the Silk Road Marketplace](https://blockchain.info/tx/ba4d94b72fbaa200e7be1e4351f694e5eb6bb85875e06e8a070f8a3c8a344cbc).

Six hours after entering the Marketplace, at 10:12 am, I was seized along with 29,657 of my comrades. Later that day, Ross Ulbricht was arrested at a library in San Francisco.

![](/assets/images/2015/m5/hello-i-am-a-bitcoin5.png)

*Silk Road on October 2*

The FBI or whatever transferred us to an address for [Silkroad Seized Coins](https://blockchain.info/address/1F1tAaz5x1HUXrCNLbtMDqcw6o5GNn4xqX?offset=450&filter=0). And there we sat and waited for a really long time. Lots of people sent micropayments to our address, with words of encouragement and spam and phishing attempts attached.

The following June, we were moved to a new location for the [US Marshals auction](https://blockchain.info/tx/7ecad9a3e67bebda52c0bdafb53863938b86e30e58eb179aec747903b50baa2d) and divided into 3000-BTC blocks.

That was a rough few weeks as I envisioned a future providing liquidity for SecondMarket or some such. The price of bitcoin was over $600 by then; who else would want a $1.8M auction block?

As it turns out, [Tim Draper wanted all ten auction blocks](https://blockchain.info/tx/9e95c3c3c96f57527cdc649550bf8e92892f7651f718d846033798aee333b0c3). On July 1, we moved to Tim’s [account on Vaurum](https://blockchain.info/address/1a8LDh3qtCdMFAgRXzMrdvB8w1EG4h1Xi) (now called Mirror).

And [here I am today](https://blockchain.info/address/3Hs6BYdun3t2A7eteDkBwnFN1rQX53rSYQ). I don’t know what comes next, but I feel in my heart that fate includes a trip to [Elaine’s bitcoin wallet](https://blockchain.info/address/1ELaiNeQk4rpAL2dUYJ9MSm4dzwo7xG4E5). You see, I was always disappointed in the timing of the Silk Road seizure. I had only just arrived in the marketplace! I didn’t even have the opportunity to partake in a trade for cocaine or murder-for-hire or even just some damn porn. But now, maybe I will.

*It is easy to trace bitcoin transactions, but difficult to determine if they actually reflect real-world dealings.
 By the way, if you use bitcoin, you should really generate a new address for each transaction. I would know, because I am a bitcoin.
