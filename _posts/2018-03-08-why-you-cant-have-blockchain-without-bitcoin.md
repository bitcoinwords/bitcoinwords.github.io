Here is a perfect illustration of why you can’t have Blockchain without Bitcoin.

![](/assets/images/2018/m3/why-you-cant-have-blockchain-without-bitcoin4.jpg)

This is an abacus. The frame is the block chain. The Bitcoin is the counters. Without the frame, you can’t have ordered counters (Bitcoin). Without the counters, you have…just a frame.
