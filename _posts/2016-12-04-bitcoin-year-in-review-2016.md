# [Bitcoin Year-In-Review 2016](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016)
### By [Pete Dushenski](http://www.contravex.com/)
### Posted December 4, 2016

As with our [2014 Edition](https://contravex.com/2014/11/24/bitcoin-year-in-review-2014-the-price-went-down-so-we-prepared-for-war/) and somewhat stillborn [2015 Edition](https://contravex.com/2016/01/21/an-historical-timeline-of-the-real-bitcoin-trb-development-part-ii/), it’s that time of year again when we review the past twelve months and take stock of what [The Most Serene Republic of Bitcoin](https://contravex.com/2014/11/04/lets-cut-to-the-chase-is-la-serenissima-a-cult/) has accomplished. Without further ado :

* USD lost 40% of its value relative to BTC.
* Network difficulty and hashrate increased nearly 3x.<sup>[i](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_0_16986)</sup>
* The block reward halved for only the second time.<sup>[ii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_1_16986)</sup>
* BitBet entered receivership.<sup>[iii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_2_16986)</sup>
* Chinese mining cartel busted politically.<sup>[iv](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_3_16986)</sup>
* MPEx went private.<sup>[v](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_4_16986)</sup>
* S.MPIF closed.<sup>[vi](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_5_16986)</sup>
* Court transitioned from #bitcoin-assets to #trilema following BitBet schism.<sup>[vii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_6_16986)</sup>
* Redundant logs implemented.<sup>[viii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_7_16986)</sup>
* First Republican block explorer / slicer released.<sup>[ix](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_8_16986)</sup>
* Raft of new bots introduced in-channel.<sup>[x](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_9_16986)</sup>
* T – ticket management system released.<sup>[xi](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_10_16986)</sup>
* New WoT visualiser and browser released.<sup>[xii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_11_16986)</sup>
* No Such lAbs (NSA) unveiled FUCKGOATS entropy dongle.<sup>[xiii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_12_16986)</sup>
* Republican mailhole released.<sup>[xiv](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_13_16986)</sup>
* Altcoins and their pimply pumpers frankly continued to fizzle.<sup>[xv](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_14_16986)</sup>
* The Real Bitcoin Foundation celebrated its second birthday and officially released client.<sup>[xvi](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_15_16986)</sup>
* GPG parachute designed.<sup>[xvii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_16_16986)</sup>
* AMD hardware debugger unbricked.<sup>[xviii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_17_16986)</sup>
* Hanbot wrote the best Elliot Rodger-themed fanfic.<sup>[xix](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_18_16986)</sup>
* Eulora lifted off.<sup>[xx](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_19_16986)</sup>
* Unicode badly bludgeoned.<sup>[xxi](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_20_16986)</sup>
* The Deep / Dark Web officially defined.<sup>[xxii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_21_16986)</sup>
* DNS fell out of favour while, ironically, the prospect of a Republican DNS registry materialised.<sup>[xxiii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_22_16986)</sup>
* Block cipher competition announced.<sup>[xxiv](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_23_16986)</sup>
* Courts Circus competition winners announced.<sup>[xxv](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_24_16986)</sup>
* Jokes were had.<sup>[xxvi](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_25_16986)</sup>
* TMSR participation levels made explicit.<sup>[xxvii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_26_16986)</sup>
* Leaner and meaner Lordship list announced.<sup>[xxviii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_27_16986)</sup>
* Gossipd spec updated.<sup>[xxix](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_28_16986)</sup>
* The SFYL playbook formalised.<sup>[xxx](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_29_16986)</sup>
* Potentially very, very silly WoT bet made.<sup>[xxxi](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_30_16986)</sup>
* Phuctor broke a lot more RSA keys than anyone expected.<sup>[xxxii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_31_16986)</sup>
* Bubble bullet dodged.<sup>[xxxiii](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_32_16986)</sup>
* Republic selected upcoming US President.<sup>[xxxiv](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#footnote_33_16986)</sup>

See you in another twelve short months!

1. Difficulty increased from 103`880`340`815 to 286`765`766`821 while hashrate increased from 743`604`444 GH/s to 2`052`749`317 GH/s.[↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_0_16986)
2. As of July, miners are now awarded only 12.5 BTC plus tx fees, down from 25 BTC, which was the rate for roughly the last four years. It was 50BTC ~every ten minutes prior to that. [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_1_16986)
3. The foremost perimutuel betting website in Bitcoin was dissolved by its founding partners following a [contractual dispute](http://trilema.com/2016/the-greatly-anticipated-bitbet-sbbet-february-2016-statement/). I then lost a pretty thrilling [live auction](https://contravex.com/2016/04/07/that-time-i-let-outright-ownership-of-bitbet-slip-through-my-fingers/) for the tainted bones. [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_2_16986)
4. Apparently the [miners](http://qntra.net/2016/03/a-miner-problem/) don’t control Bitcoin after all. Who knew ? [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_3_16986)
5. This resulted in the largest (~250kbtc) and cleanest unwinding of stock issuance in the history of Bitcoin when [S.MPOE](https://contravex.com/2015/07/08/s-mpoe-traders-delight/) was [delisted](http://trilema.com/2016/mpex-smpoe-closing-statement/). [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_4_16986)
6. Turns out that investing in anything Bitcoin-related that isn’t Bitcoin proper is [no mean feat](http://trilema.com/2016/mpif-fmpif-closing-statement/). [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_5_16986)
7. kakobrekla, punkman, and Funkenstein_ were the most notable stragglers, the rest move forward, onwards, and upwards unflinchingly.[↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_6_16986)
8. Public-facing channel logging had previously been dependent on kako, which obviously couldn’t stand going forward, so [phf](http://btcbase.org/log/) and [Framedragger](http://log.mkj.lt/trilema/) stepped up and released their own public log sites, each with differing formats. Redundancy and complementarity FTW! [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_7_16986)
9. Our man Ben Vulpes released the mythologically-named [Mimisbrunnr](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/mimisbrunnr.cascadianhacker.com). [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_8_16986)
10. The [bots directory](http://bots.contravex.com) has them all listed with their ever-expanding functionalities. This past twelve months has seen Vietnam War-levels of explosions in bot capability and scope. Trinque even made vpatches for an [IRC bot](http://trinque.org/2016/08/10/ircbot-genesis/) and a [log bot](http://trinque.org/2016/08/11/logbot-genesis/). [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_9_16986)
11. mod6 made this [spiffy little tool](http://therealbitcoin.org/ml/btc-dev/2016-May/000223.html) for keeping track of TMSR software development projects and objectives. [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_10_16986)
12. Old maintainer mike_c was lost to meatspace commitments as near as anyone can decipher. So trinque, who also maintains deedbot, took charge and crafted a [replacement](http://wot.deedbot.org). [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_11_16986)
13. Entropy is a tricky business, [as we know](https://contravex.com/2014/03/14/on-making-high-entropy-bitcoin-paper-wallets/), so 0.028BTC for [FUCKGOATS](http://archive.is/hXB4r) is an exceedingly modest sum for an auditable and WoT-manufactured source. Particularly as more whitening “[bugs](http://qntra.net/2016/08/rng-whitening-bug-weakened-all-versions-of-gpg/)” come to light. [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_12_16986)
14. Ben Vulpes at it [again](http://cascadianhacker.com/ann-a-mailhole)! [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_13_16986)
15. [Zcash](https://contravex.com/2016/01/25/zcash-will-crash-just-like-gavincoin-garzikcoin-xt-segwit-and-classic-now-you-know/) sputtered and started like an old VW diesel, Ethereum and its [unscrupulous founders](https://contravex.com/2016/06/29/from-the-scammer-files-anthony-di-iorio/) flopped about like fish out of water, and “[Classic](http://qntra.net/2016/01/gavin-and-toomim-present-classic-another-hard-fork-risk/)” / “XTcoin” / “Hard fork” / “Soft fork that’s really a hard fork” all sucked a dead donkey. Bitcoin 2.0… what was [that](https://contravex.com/2014/03/19/there-is-no-bitcoin-2-0/) again ? [↩](http://www.contravex.com/2016/12/04/bitcoin-year-in-review-2016#identifier_14_16986)
